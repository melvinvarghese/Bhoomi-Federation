National Calendar

The national workweek and public holiday are established by the Adoption of the Official National Calendar, issued on 7 June 2025.

Weekly schedule

Working days

Monday to Friday

Non-working days

Saturday and Sunday

BHF/005/2025 · Adoption of the Official National Calendar

National Day

5 June

The National Calendar names 5 June as the sole public holiday and provides that government offices remain closed.

BHF/005/2025 · Adoption of the Official National Calendar

Annual observance

The National Day Proclamation establishes that 5 June shall be observed annually. The founding and National Day proclamations are both dated 5 June 2025; neither specifies which was issued first that day.
