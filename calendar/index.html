The listed public holiday

5 June · National Day

BHF/005/2025 calls 5 June the sole public holiday and says government offices remain closed.

Primary recordSource S5 · BHF/005/2025

The annual observance

The separate National Day Proclamation says 5 June shall be observed annually. That recurrence is stated in BHF/002/2025 and BHF/003/2025; it is not text from BHF/005/2025.
