About & History

A short record, with open edges.

Five documents set out the Bhoomi Federation’s public story in June 2025. The sequence is clear by date, except that two records share 5 June and specify no order between them.

The 2025 record

  1. 4 June 2025 · BHF/001/2025

    Flag Adoption Proclamation

    The proclamation describes a vertical blue-white-green triband. Its text extraction and Archive.org OCR metadata disagree on the green value; no exact-color flag is reproduced here.

    Primary recordSource S1

  2. 5 June 2025 · BHF/002/2025

    National Day Proclamation

    Names 5 June as the Federation’s inception and says it is to be observed annually as National Day.

    Primary recordSource S2Source S3

  3. 5 June 2025 · BHF/003/2025

    Founding Proclamation

    Declares the Federation formally established, names 5 June 2025 as its foundation date, and states four values: clarity of thought, ethical governance, dignity of individual existence, and collective peace.

    Primary recordSource S3

  4. 6 June 2025 · BHF/004/2025

    A demonym is designated

    The record names people granted citizenship under its stated framework as Bhoomians. It does not define eligibility or a grant process.

    Primary recordSource S4

  5. 7 June 2025 · BHF/005/2025

    A national calendar is adopted

    The printed title is “Adoption of the Official National Calendar.” The “Executive Order 2” label appears in Archive.org metadata and the filename, not in that title.

    Primary recordSource S5

BHF/002/2025 and BHF/003/2025 are both dated 5 June 2025. The documents do not establish which came first that day.

The earlier public vision

The former public website spoke of dignity, justice, welfare, ethical governance, universal welfare, and philosophical leadership. These are historical public-facing statements, not a detailed constitution or an enacted policy programme.

Unresolved leads, not timeline entries
