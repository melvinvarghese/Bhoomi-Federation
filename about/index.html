About & History

The Federation’s founding

The first national records were issued in June 2025. They established the Federation’s founding date and values, national flag, citizenship designation, and calendar.

National history

  1. 4 June 2025 · BHF/001/2025

    National Flag

    The Flag Adoption Proclamation establishes a vertical blue-white-green triband and records its colours, proportions, symbolism and protocols of use.

    Read the Flag Adoption Proclamation

  2. 5 June 2025 · BHF/002/2025

    National Day

    The National Day Proclamation identifies 5 June as the Federation’s inception and establishes its annual observance as National Day.

    Read the National Day Proclamation

  3. 5 June 2025 · BHF/003/2025

    Founding Proclamation

    The Founding Proclamation formally establishes the Federation, names 5 June 2025 as its foundation date, and sets out four values: clarity of thought, ethical governance, dignity of individual existence, and collective peace.

    Read the Founding Proclamation

  4. 6 June 2025 · BHF/004/2025

    Citizenship designation

    The record designates people granted citizenship under the stated framework as Bhoomians. The 2025 record does not set out eligibility or a grant process; the Citizenship and Identity Act, BHF/009/2026, establishes those rules on 3 November 2026.

    Read the citizenship designation

  5. 7 June 2025 · BHF/005/2025

    National Calendar

    The Adoption of the Official National Calendar lists Monday through Friday as working days, Saturday and Sunday as non-working days, and 5 June as the sole public holiday. Government offices remain closed on that day.

    Read the Adoption of the Official National Calendar

BHF/002/2025 and BHF/003/2025 are both dated 5 June 2025. Neither record establishes which was issued first that day.
