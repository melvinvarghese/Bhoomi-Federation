2026 · New fictional canon

A new layer, kept separate.

This registry contains four newly established additions to the Bhoomi Federation’s fictional canon, all dated 3 November 2026. They develop a constitutional and civic foundation without changing, backdating, or pretending to be part of the original 2025 records.

Framework only. These are fictional design records with no real-world legal effect. They make no claim of real-world statehood or recognition, and they do not describe an operating government, court, citizen registry, or application service.

The territory and number or names of future member units remain unspecified; the member-unit schedule is intentionally blank. Each record below is a new fictional canon record, not a 2025 record.

The four foundation records

New fictional canon · framework only

  1. BHF/006/2026

    Constitution of the Bhoomi Federation

    Sets the fictional rights floor, civic-federal structure, elected Assembly, executive accountability, independent courts, emergency safeguards, and amendment rules. It names the founder-specific, non-elective Chief Architect as Head of Country. After the founder’s tenure, that office remains permanently vacant; the Assembly then supervises collectively without becoming a successor Head of Country.
