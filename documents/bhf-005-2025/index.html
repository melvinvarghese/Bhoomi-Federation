Issued by
Melvin Varghese, Chief Architect, The Bhoomi Federation

Weekly schedule and public holiday

Monday through Friday are working days. Saturday and Sunday are non-working days. The record names 5 June as the sole public holiday and states that government offices remain closed on that day.

National Day

5 June is the sole public holiday under this calendar. The annual observance is established by BHF/002/2025 and BHF/003/2025.
