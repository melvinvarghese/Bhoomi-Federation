    BHF/004/2025

  5. Adoption of the Official National Calendar

    BHF/005/2025

Constitutional records issued in 2026

3 November 2026

The Constitution and three accompanying laws were issued on 3 November 2026. Their issue dates are distinct from the original records of 2025.

  1. BHF/006/2026

    Constitution of the Bhoomi Federation

    Establishes the Federation’s rights, federal structure, elected Assembly, courts, emergency safeguards and amendment rules.

    Read the Constitution

  2. BHF/007/2026

    Federal Institutions and Accountability Act

    Defines the legislature, executive, courts and independent public institutions, including collective Assembly supervision after the Chief Architect’s tenure.

    Read the institutional provisions

  3. BHF/008/2026

    Public Law and Administration Act

    Sets the legal hierarchy, public notice and records-access duties, reasoned administrative decisions, judicial remedies and audit requirements.

    Read the public law provisions

  4. BHF/009/2026

    Citizenship and Identity Act

    Defines citizenship by descent, birth and naturalisation, identity protections, review procedures and safeguards against statelessness.

    Read the citizenship provisions
