Official Documents · five original records

Read the records at their source.

These five records are dated between 4 and 7 June 2025. Their entries remain visible without JavaScript; each source link opens the Archive.org record.

Archive.org displays CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for these items. The listing does not independently establish redistribution authority, so this site links to the source rather than hosting copies.

  1. Flag Adoption Proclamation

    BHF/001/2025

    Adopts a vertical blue-white-green triband. The exact green value remains disputed between PDF text extraction and Archive.org OCR metadata.

    Primary recordSource S1

    Open Archive.org record

    4 June 2025

  2. National Day Proclamation

    BHF/002/2025

    Identifies 5 June as inception and says the date shall be observed annually as National Day.

    Primary recordSource S2

    Open Archive.org record

    5 June 2025

  3. Founding Proclamation

    BHF/003/2025

    Declares the Federation formally established and names 5 June 2025 as its foundation date. The records do not establish which of the two 5 June proclamations came first that day.

    Primary recordSource S3

    Lists four values: clarity of thought, ethical governance, dignity of individual existence, and collective peace.

    Primary recordSource S3

    Open Archive.org record

    5 June 2025

  4. On the Official Designation of Citizens of the Bhoomi Federation

    BHF/004/2025

    Designates people granted citizenship under the stated framework as Bhoomians; it does not define eligibility or a grant process.

    Primary recordSource S4

    Open Archive.org record

    6 June 2025

  5. Adoption of the Official National Calendar

    BHF/005/2025

    Lists Monday–Friday as working days, weekends as non-working days, and 5 June as the sole public holiday. “Executive Order 2” is a metadata/filename label.

    Primary recordSource S5

    Open Archive.org record

    7 June 2025
