Issued by

Citizenship designation

The record designates people granted citizenship under the Federation’s stated legal and administrative framework as Bhoomians.

The 2025 record does not define eligibility or a grant process. The Citizenship and Identity Act, BHF/009/2026, issued on 3 November 2026, sets out those rules.
