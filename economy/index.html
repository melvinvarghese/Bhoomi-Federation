Annual income tax

Proposed Bill · 4 November 2026

The Annual Income Tax Bill is proposed for consideration by both chambers of the Federal Legislature. It is not yet in force.

If passed by both chambers, the Act would take effect on publication in the public register.

The proposed Bill sets annual income tax at 10 per cent of each adult’s annual income. A minor is exempt. A student without a job is exempt.

  • If annual income is 10, tax would be 1 in the same unit of account.
  • If annual income is zero, no income tax would be due.

The Bill does not yet define annual income or the assessment, filing, payment and collection process. Those details remain to be completed before introduction.

Proposed Bill text

Annual Income Tax Bill

A Bill to provide for an annual tax on income and for related matters.

To be considered by both chambers of the Federal Legislature. If passed by both chambers, this Bill shall be enacted as follows.

1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Annual Income Tax Act.

2. Public declaration

The Legislature declares that everyone in the country has what they need, no one is poor, and the country is happy.

3. Annual income tax

  1. Subject to the exemptions in section 4, each adult shall pay an income tax for each year equal to 10 per cent of that adult’s annual income for that year.
  2. Where annual income is 10, the tax is 1.
  3. Where annual income is zero, no income tax is payable under this Act.

4. Exemptions

No income tax is payable under this Act by a minor, or by a student who has no job.

5. Matters requiring completion

Before introduction, define any necessary terms, including “annual income,” “adult,” “minor,” “student,” and “job,” and add any assessment, filing, payment, collection or administrative procedures. This Bill sets no age threshold, income-base definition or procedure.

6. Chief Architect’s authority

Nothing in this Act limits or diminishes the Chief Architect’s supreme authority above the Constitution and all institutions.

7. Commencement

This Act enters into force on the date of its publication in the public register after passage by both chambers.
